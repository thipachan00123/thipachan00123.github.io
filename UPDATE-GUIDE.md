# 📝 Portfolio Update Guide — Simple Steps for Non-Coders

This guide will help you update your portfolio without touching any code.

---

## 🔑 Access Your Admin Panel

Open your browser and go to:

```
https://sarwarjony.github.io/admin.html
```

Or locally: just open `admin.html` in your browser.

---

## 👤 Update Your Profile

**When to use:** When you change your email, bio, photo, or social links.

1. Click **Profile** in the left sidebar
2. Update your information:
   - **Full Name**: Your name
   - **Professional Title**: Your headline
   - **Short Bio**: 2-3 sentences about you
   - **Email**: Your contact email
   - **Photo URL**: Link to your profile photo
   - **CV URL**: Link to your CV PDF
3. Add your social profile URLs:
   - LinkedIn, GitHub, Google Scholar, ORCID, ResearchGate
4. Click **Save Profile**
5. Click **Preview Site** to see changes

---

## 🗺️ Add a New Project

**When to use:** When you complete a GIS project, map, or analysis.

1. Click **Projects** in the sidebar
2. Click **Add Project** button (blue button, top right)
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Title**: Project name
   - **Category**: GIS / Remote Sensing / Urban Planning / etc.
   - **Status**: Completed / Ongoing / Planned
   - **Date**: Year (e.g., 2024)
   - **Description**: What the project is about (2-3 sentences)
   - **Tools**: Comma-separated (e.g., ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, Python)
   - **Tags**: Keywords (e.g., Flood Risk, Spatial Analysis)
   - **Thumbnail URL**: Image URL for the project card
   - **GitHub URL**: Your GitHub repository link
   - **Report URL**: Link to your project report
   - **Featured**: Check this to show on homepage
4. Click **Save**

---

## 🔬 Add Research

**When to use:** When you start or complete a research project.

1. Click **Research** in the sidebar
2. Click **Add Research**
3. Fill in:
   - **Title**: Research title
   - **Research Area**: e.g., Flood Risk & Spatial Analysis
   - **Status**: Idea / Ongoing / Under Review / Published / Completed
   - **Description**: What the research is about
   - **Research Question**: The main question you're answering
   - **Methodology**: Your research approach
   - **Tools**: Software you're using
   - **Keywords**: Important terms
4. Click **Save**

---

## 📜 Add a Certificate

**When to use:** When you complete an online course or earn a certification.

1. Click **Certificates** in the sidebar
2. Click **Add Certificate**
3. Fill in:
   - **Certificate Name**: Full certificate name
   - **Issuing Organization**: Who gave you the certificate
   - **Category**: GIS / Remote Sensing / Programming / etc.
   - **Issue Date**: Month and Year
   - **Credential URL**: Verification link (from Coursera, ESRI, etc.)
   - **Certificate Image URL**: Image of the certificate
4. Check **Featured** for important certificates
5. Click **Save**

---

## 💼 Add Experience

**When to use:** When you start a new internship, job, or volunteer role.

1. Click **Experience** in the sidebar
2. Click **Add Experience**
3. Fill in:
   - **Position**: Your job title
   - **Organization**: Company/institution name
   - **Employment Type**: Internship / Academic / Volunteer / etc.
   - **Start Date**: Month + Year
   - **Current**: Check if you still work there
   - **Description**: What you do in this role
   - **Responsibilities**: Key duties (one per line)
   - **Skills Used**: Comma-separated skills
4. Click **Save**

---

## 📚 Add a Publication

**When to use:** When you publish or submit a paper.

1. Click **Publications** in the sidebar
2. Click **Add Publication**
3. Fill in:
   - **Title**: Full paper title
   - **Authors**: All authors (e.g., Jony, S., Ahmed, K., et al.)
   - **Journal/Conference**: Where it's published
   - **Year**: Publication year
   - **Type**: Journal Article / Conference Paper / Thesis
   - **Abstract**: Brief summary
   - **DOI**: Digital Object Identifier (if published)
   - **Paper URL**: Link to the paper
4. Click **Save**

---

## 🏆 Add Achievement

**When to use:** When you win an award or achieve something notable.

1. Click **Achievements** in the sidebar
2. Click **Add Achievement**
3. Fill in:
   - **Title**: Achievement name
   - **Organization**: Who recognized you
   - **Category**: Award / Competition / Academic / etc.
   - **Date**: Month + Year
   - **Icon**: Choose an emoji (🏆 🥇 🎖️ 📜 🌟)
   - **Description**: Details about the achievement

## 🔗 Update Social Links

**When to use:** When you create new academic profiles.

1. Click **Social Links** in the sidebar
2. Find each platform (LinkedIn, GitHub, etc.)
3. Paste your profile URL
4. Check **Show** to display it on your website
5. Click **Save Social Links**

---

## 💾 Backup Your Data

**Do this regularly to avoid losing your content!**

1. Click **Backup / Export** in the sidebar
2. Click **Export Portfolio Data**
3. A JSON file will download to your computer
4. Save this file safely (Google Drive, email yourself, etc.)

**To restore from backup:**
1. Click **Import Data**
2. Select your backup JSON file
3. Confirm the import

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: My changes disappeared after clearing browser history!**
A: This happens when you clear browser data. Always export your backup before clearing anything! Import your backup file to restore.

**Q: How do I add my photo?**
A: Upload your photo to your GitHub repository, then copy the image URL and paste it in Admin → Profile → Photo URL.

**Q: How do I update my CV?**
A: Upload your CV PDF to GitHub (assets/documents/cv.pdf), then paste the URL in Admin → Profile → CV URL.

**Q: Can someone else see my admin panel?**
A: Anyone who knows the URL can access it. The URL is not publicly linked, which provides basic obscurity. For sensitive portfolios, consider adding proper authentication.

**Q: How do I make changes permanent on GitHub?**
A: Export your data from Admin → Backup, then upload the updated JSON files to your GitHub repository.

## 📞 Need Help?

If you're stuck, the safest approach is always:
1. Export your current data (backup)
2. Make your changes
3. Preview the result
4. Export again as a new backup
