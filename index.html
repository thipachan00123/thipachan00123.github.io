About Me

Urban Planner & GIS Specialist

I am a passionate Urban & Regional Planning student at Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET), with a deep focus on Geographic Information Systems (GIS), Remote Sensing, and Spatial Data Analysis. My academic journey is driven by the desire to understand and solve complex urban challenges through the power of geospatial technology.

My research interests center on flood risk assessment, climate resilience, and disaster risk reduction — areas where spatial analysis can make a real-world difference. I am actively seeking research collaborations and international opportunities to expand my academic and professional horizon.
